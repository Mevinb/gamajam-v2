# After the Still Water — riverside chapter

Finish the original fourteen missions and reach the lake. Choose **Live quietly (Peace)** or **Become the world (Dominion)**.
After its resolution, the summary offers **Continue · After the lake**. This is a deliberate continuation; waiting at the lake
or at the summary does not choose a future or advance to the new chapter.

The chapter keeps the first outcome and campaign statistics. A chapter checkpoint restores completed objectives, but not
the bodies holding cooperative consoles. The free amoeba returns at the checkpoint and must find hosts again. Checkpoints
persist within this game session; they are not a disk save.

## Mission 15 — After the Still Water

You arrive at a riverside landing, beyond the old city. Petrov's emergency receiver is in the open-front landing office.
Borrow a nearby host, face the receiver and hold **E** to hear her distress signal. The mission waypoints point to each control.

Take the maintenance engineer near flood control. Hold **E** at the bypass for six seconds to isolate the polluted intake.
Work stops if you leave its range or enter a pursuit. Finishing the floodgate creates a checkpoint.

Peace treats this as a rescue undertaken despite the promise of a quiet life. Dominion treats it as the beginning of a wider network.

## Mission 16 — Borrowed Hearts / A Willing Network

Possess a scientist at Petrov's field clinic and analyse the runoff sample for seven seconds. The scientist's profession
matters; a guard or maintenance worker cannot operate the analysis equipment.

Use **V** to split if only one amoeba is present. Possess a guard with one amoeba, operate shelter dispatch with **E**, and
leave that guard at the console. Press **Tab** to switch to the other amoeba, borrow a scientist, and operate the records
terminal for eight seconds. The same host cannot work both controls. Moving the supporting host away cancels the pair.

Peace removes identifying records to protect the evacuees. Dominion acquires them to recruit the settlement. Both branches
open the corridor and checkpoint its completion. A reset requires rearming any cooperative console that was still in progress.

## Mission 17 — The Open Channel / The First Many

Take a maintenance host to the mast power stabiliser. Activate it and keep that host there while the other amoeba works the
radio. On Peace, the transmitting host may be a civilian or scientist; on Dominion, use a guard or scientist. The transmission
takes ten seconds and must remain free of pursuit. The radio cordon is guarded, so plan your approach and borrowed role.

Peace sends an anonymous relief call and the antidote formula. Dominion seeds a neural relay. Completing the transmission
creates another checkpoint. A cancelled attempt can be rearmed and retried without replaying earlier completed objectives.

Go to the south ferry waypoint. Release every borrowed host before leaving: **Q** frees the active body, then **Tab** and
**Q** free any other occupied host. Switch back to the free amoeba at the ferry and use its intake with **E**. A pending
possession leap must also finish before departure.
The final chapter summary reflects the original chosen future. The old presentation shortcut does not replay the lake from here.

## Quick recovery checks

- Missing role: the landing, flood control, clinic, dispatch and mast each have nearby suitable people.
- Console refuses: inspect its prompt for profession, prerequisite, range, pursuit or paired-host requirements.
- Paired work cancels: leave the supporting host in range and use a different amoeba and host for the working terminal.
- After a checkpoint reload: completed flags remain; find new hosts and rearm transient supporting controls.
- Ferry refuses: finish the radio transmission, release all hosts with Tab/Q, lose pursuit, then use the gangway to reach
  the intake at the front of the ferry deck. The prompt identifies any remaining occupied host or pending leap.
