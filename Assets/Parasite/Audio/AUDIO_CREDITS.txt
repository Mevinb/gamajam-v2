PARASITE — audio sources and licences (generated by Tools/audio/*.sh; see Tools/audio/*.py docstrings)

Music (Resources/Audio/Music, Sfx/ending_sting): original procedural score composed and rendered offline with
numpy (Tools/audio/gen_music.py). No samples. Project-owned.

Voice-over (Resources/Audio/Voice) and gameplay vocals (Resources/Audio/Vocals): synthesised offline with
Kokoro-82M v1.0 (hexgrad, https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) — model weights and voice packs licensed
Apache-2.0 (commercial use permitted). Voices used: bm_george, bf_emma, af_nicole+af_sky, af_heart, am_michael,
am_fenrir, am_onyx, bm_lewis, am_eric, af_kore, am_liam, af_sarah, bf_isabella, bm_daniel, af_bella, af_jessica,
am_puck, am_adam, af_nova. G2P: misaki (Apache-2.0), espeak-ng via espeakng-loader (GPL-3.0 tool, used offline only,
not shipped).

Kenney audio packs (CC0 1.0, https://kenney.nl — see KENNEY_LICENSE.txt): Impact Sounds, Interface Sounds,
Sci-fi Sounds, Digital Audio, RPG Audio — processed into footsteps, UI, doors, terminals, slime/impact layers.

OpenGameArt.org (CC0 1.0):
  "High traffic road sounds" by IgnasD — https://opengameart.org/content/high-traffic-road-sounds (city bed)
  "Park Ambiences" by Thimras — https://opengameart.org/content/park-ambiences (wind in city bed, dusk bird chirps)
  "Dog barking mono" by Brandon Morris (submitted by HaelDB), dual OGA-BY 3.0 / CC0 (used under CC0) —
      https://opengameart.org/content/dog-barking-mono (distant dog barks)

Everything else (rain, lab hum, tunnel, fountain, drain, steam, AC unit, car engine/tyres, CCTV servo, sirens,
thunder, horns, drips, beeps, squelches, whooshes, chimes) is synthesised in Tools/audio/gen_ambience.py and
gen_sfx.py. Project-owned.
