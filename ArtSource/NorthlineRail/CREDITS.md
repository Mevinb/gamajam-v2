# Northline rail authorship

The eleven rail assemblies, UV layout, manufacturing details and authoring recipe are original PARASITE project work. No downloaded models, photographs, logos, fonts or third-party surface maps were added by this kit.

The unchanged `CityGatePilot_SharedPBR` material and its three original procedural atlas images are reused from the existing [gate source package](../CityGatePilot/CREDITS.md). All three original image byte hashes are pinned in the production manifest and verified against packed image data. No new runtime textures are introduced by this source promotion.

Blender 4.5.16 LTS performed actual authoring, FBX export/import and CPU review rendering. The Blender executable is not bundled. These project-authored files follow the repository's licensing terms; this note adds no independent third-party licence or broader ownership claim for the game.

The review images use studio lighting and remain in ignored Logs. They do not establish native game appearance, RTX rendering or gameplay/performance acceptance.
